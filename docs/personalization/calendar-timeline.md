# Calendar and Timeline

## MVP scope

Extract dates explicitly present in public pages and display them as a timeline. Do not infer private appointments or future behavior.

## Checklist

- [ ] Extract date and event text.
- [ ] Preserve source URL.
- [ ] Distinguish past, present, and undated items.
- [ ] Mark ambiguous dates.
- [ ] Render a chronological list.

Do not build calendar-provider integrations during the six-hour sprint.
